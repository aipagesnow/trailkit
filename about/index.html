About Trailkit

Trailkit is a gear guide for people who already know the activity and need the product that fits a constraint: budget, weight, weather, width, or trip length.

We are not a lab. Seed pages name that limit. When a model is illustrative, the page says so.

The working name lives in site.config.json and js/site.js.
