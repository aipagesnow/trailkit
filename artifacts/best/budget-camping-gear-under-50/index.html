Best budget camping gear under $50

Updated October 5, 2026 · 7 min read · Trailkit Editors

Spend under $50 on things that are simple: a backup stove, a dry bag, a sit pad, a repair kit, a picnic wash bin. Do not spend under $50 expecting a backpacking tent or a winter bag. Those categories fail in predictable ways. The BRS stove is a fair cheap backup. A headlamp under $50 is fine if it has a lock and a low mode. For the real shelter, use the tent guide and buy once.

Who this is for.

Car campers filling gaps and backpackers who need a cheap spare, not a full kit from a single discount aisle.

FAQ

What should I never cheap out on?

Shelter in bad weather, a sleep system for the real temperature, and footwear.

Are Amazon basics stoves safe?

A canister stove used as directed, outside, is a normal tool. A stove used in a tent is not.

Is a $50 sleeping bag enough?

For a warm summer car camp, maybe. For backpacking near freezing, no.

Where do real buys live?

On the tents, bags, boots, and stove pages, where the constraint is not a price cap.
